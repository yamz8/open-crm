# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about the code, not the person writing it.

## What we expect

- **Be direct and be kind.** Blunt technical feedback is welcome; contempt is not. "This
  query will table-scan on large datasets" is useful. "Did you even think about this?" is not.
- **Assume competence.** People come to this project with different backgrounds. Someone
  unfamiliar with SQLite internals or MCP is not thereby unqualified to contribute.
- **Accept that you may be wrong.** Including maintainers. A well-argued objection is a gift.
- **Respect people's time.** Search before filing, read the error's `hint` before asking, and
  say what you already tried.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment of any kind; demeaning or discriminatory comments about someone's identity,
background, or ability; sexual attention or imagery; deliberate intimidation, stalking, or
doxxing; sustained disruption of discussion; and encouraging any of the above.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments, and any other project
space, in public and in private.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainers privately — open a
[private report](https://github.com/yamz8/open-crm/security/advisories/new) or message
[@yamz8](https://github.com/yamz8) directly on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
Include what happened, where, and any links.

You will get an acknowledgement within 3 working days. We will look into it and tell you what
we decided. If your report concerns a maintainer, say so, and it will be handled by someone
else.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and pattern, maintainers may:

1. give a private warning explaining what was wrong,
2. ask for a public apology,
3. temporarily block someone from participating, or
4. permanently ban them from the project.

Maintainers who do not enforce this in good faith may themselves be removed.

## Scope

This applies to everyone in project spaces — contributors, maintainers, and people just
passing through — and to anyone representing the project in public.

---

This document is intentionally short and specific to this project. If you want a fuller,
widely adopted alternative, the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org)
is a good one; nothing here is meant to conflict with its spirit.
